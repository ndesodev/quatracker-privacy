Privacy
Policy

NdesoDev · Aqua Tracker  ·  Effective: June 2025

NdesoDev ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information through the Aqua Tracker mobile application.
01

Information We Collect

  • User Profile DataWhen you set up the app, we may collect information such as your gender and body weight to calculate your daily hydration goal.
  • Activity DataWe track your water intake records, including the type of drink, amount, and timestamp.
  • Account InformationIf you choose to sign in with Google, we collect your basic profile information (email address and name) provided by Google to sync your data across devices.
  • Health Connect Data (Optional)If you explicitly grant permission, the app may read or write hydration data to Android Health Connect to help you consolidate your health information.
02

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the service, including tracking your daily water intake.
  • To calculate personalized hydration goals based on your physical profile.
  • To sync your data across your devices via Firebase Cloud.
  • To send periodic reminders to keep you hydrated throughout the day.
03

Data Storage & Security

  • Local StorageYour data is stored locally on your device using a secure database.
  • Cloud StorageIf you are signed in, your data is securely synced to Firebase (Google Cloud).
  • Health ConnectData shared with Health Connect is subject to the Health Connect Permissions and Google's privacy standards.
04

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to power Aqua Tracker:

Firebase
Authentication, database syncing, and basic analytics via Google Cloud.
Google Sign-In
Facilitates secure account access using your Google credentials.
Health Connect
Integrates with other health apps on your Android device.
05

Your Rights & Control

  • Data DeletionYou can delete all your records and account data at any time via the "Reset All Data" option in the app settings.
  • Permission ManagementYou can revoke notification or Health Connect permissions at any time through your device settings.
